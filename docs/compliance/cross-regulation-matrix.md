# AssetCo AI Platform — Cross-Regulation Conflict Matrix

**Document:** Cross-Regulation Overlap and Conflict Resolution
**Scope:** EU AI Act, MiFID II/MiFIR, SEC Advisers Act, FINRA, GDPR, UK GDPR, Reg FD, MAR, SR 11-7, MAS FEAT, DORA, SFDR, SOX
**Organization:** AssetCo ($85B AUM global asset manager)
**Maintainer:** AI Governance Office / Compliance & Legal
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-20

---

## Overview

Operating across US, EU, UK, and APAC jurisdictions means AssetCo's AI applications must simultaneously satisfy regulations that were drafted independently, often with conflicting requirements. This document maps every identified conflict, documents the applicable applications, and specifies the SOE resolution — the precise mechanism by which SOE enforces a single compliant behavior that satisfies all conflicting obligations simultaneously.

Conflict categories:
- **R** — Retention: different required retention periods
- **T** — Transparency: disclosure requirements that conflict with IP or other interests
- **A** — Automation: requirements to automate vs. rights to opt out of automation
- **B** — Borders: cross-jurisdictional data sovereignty conflicts
- **I** — Information barriers: chinese wall requirements that conflict across regimes
- **M** — Model governance: overlapping model risk frameworks
