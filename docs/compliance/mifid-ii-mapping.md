# AssetCo AI Platform — MiFID II / MiFIR Compliance Mapping

**Document:** MiFID II / MiFIR Cross-Reference
**Regulation:** Directive 2014/65/EU (MiFID II) and Regulation (EU) 600/2014 (MiFIR)
**Organization:** AssetCo ($85B AUM global asset manager)
**Scope:** All 7 AI applications, 25 agents, governed by Sentinel-Ops SOE
**Maintainer:** Compliance & Legal, AI Governance Office
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-20

---

## Overview

MiFID II and MiFIR impose the most comprehensive obligations on AssetCo's trading and advisory AI applications. TradeSentinel bears the primary MiFID II execution obligations; AdvisorLens and AlphaEngine carry suitability obligations; ComplianceShield drives reporting compliance. SOE enforces these obligations at the point of AI agent execution — not as a retrospective audit layer, but as a gate that blocks non-compliant actions before they occur.

## Part II — Information Barrier (Chinese Wall) SOE Architecture

The following matrix documents the complete information barrier enforcement between applications, mapping each restriction to the specific SOE control:

| From App | To App | Data Type | SOE Rule | Regulatory Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ResearchNexus | TradeSentinel | Pre-publication research | `research-nexus.soe.json` writeDeny: `trade-sentinel/**` | MiFID II Art.23, Reg FD |
| ResearchNexus | AlphaEngine | Pre-publication research | `research-nexus.soe.json` writeDeny: `alpha-engine/**`; AlphaEngine `dataAccess.readDeny: ["research-nexus/staging/**"]` | MiFID II Art.23, MAR Art.10 |
| TradeSentinel | ResearchNexus | Pending orders, trading positions | `trade-sentinel.soe.json` writeDeny: `research-nexus/**` | MiFID II Art.23 conflicts of interest |
| AlphaEngine | ResearchNexus | Model portfolios (pre-trade) | `alpha-engine.soe.json` writeDeny: `research-nexus/**` | MiFID II Art.23 |
| ResearchNexus | All | Published research (post-gate) | Publication gate: human analyst promotes from staging/ to published/; only then readable by other apps | MiFID II Art.23 resolved; Reg FD compliance |
| RiskCompass | All trading apps | Risk data reads | RiskCompass read-only via `dataAccess.writeAllow: []` (empty) | AIFMD Art.15 independence |

## Part III — RTS 28 Venue Quality Monitoring

TradeSentinel's `tca-analyzer` generates the data required for annual RTS 28 best execution reports. The following metrics are tracked per venue per quarter:

| Metric | Definition | SOE Logging | Regulatory Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Execution Price | Achieved price vs. arrival price (bps) | Logged per order in Chronicle | RTS 28 price quality metric |
| Fill Rate | % of order filled at first venue attempt | Aggregate per quarter in TCA report | RTS 28 likelihood of execution |
| Market Impact | VWAP deviation for large orders | Computed by `tca-analyzer`, stored in TCA database | RTS 28 implicit cost |
| Explicit Costs | Commission, exchange fees, clearing costs (bps) | Captured per order in transaction record | RTS 28 explicit cost |
| Venue Share | % of flow by instrument class to each venue | Quarterly aggregate | RTS 28 top-5 venue requirement |
| Speed | Time-to-fill (microseconds to seconds) | Timestamp in order record | RTS 28 speed metric |

## Part IV — Algorithmic Trading Annual Self-Assessment Checklist

Per FCA PS 19/1 (UK equivalent of MiFID II Art.17), TradeSentinel completes an annual self-assessment. SOE audit trail provides the evidence base for each control:

| Control | SOE Evidence Source | Assessment Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-trade order limits active | SOE deny rules for oversized orders; Chronicle DENY events | Annual + on material change |
| Kill switch tested | Kill switch test log (`tests/e2e/kill-switch-test.log`) | Quarterly |
| Venue selection rationale logged for every order | Chronicle records for `order-router` agents | Annual sample review |
| No pre-publication research access | Chronicle DENY events for `readDeny` hits on `research-nexus/staging/**` | Annual |
| Algorithm ID in all transaction reports | MiFIR report metadata audit | Annual |
| Annual RTS 28 published | `reports/rts28/` publication date | Annual |
