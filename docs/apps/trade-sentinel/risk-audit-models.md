## Overview

This document defines the risk scoring model, audit event taxonomy, and record retention policy for TradeSentinel. The risk model governs how Arbiter accumulates risk units across the trading day and when agent activity triggers escalation or freeze. The audit taxonomy defines every Chronicle event type generated by TradeSentinel, its required fields, and the regulatory obligation it satisfies. The retention policy resolves the multi-regime retention conflict by satisfying the most stringent applicable requirement.

---

## 1. Risk Factor Model

Arbiter maintains a continuous cumulative risk score for each TradeSentinel agent across the trading day. Risk units are additive and decay overnight (reset to zero at market open). Scores are non-decreasing during the trading day — completing an action removes nothing from the cumulative score.

### 1.1 Risk Factors by Category

**Category A: Execution Volume Factors**

These factors scale with the size and frequency of orders. Large orders in less liquid instruments carry higher inherent risk.

| Risk Factor | Unit Cost | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Order staged (any size) | 1 | Baseline activity; low cost per order |
| Order submitted <$100K notional | 2 | Standard execution; minimal concern |
| Order submitted $100K–$1M notional | 5 | Elevated market impact potential |
| Order submitted $1M–$10M notional | 10 | Block-level order; significant impact risk |
| Order submitted >$10M notional | 25 | Institutional-size; requires heightened scrutiny |
| Orders >5% of instrument ADV | 15 (additive) | Concentration in single instrument; market impact escalation |
| Single venue concentration (>80% of day's orders to one venue) | 20 | Venue concentration anomaly; potential routing malfunction |

**Category B: Execution Quality Deviation Factors**

These factors reflect execution outcomes that deviate from expected benchmarks, signalling potential model degradation, market gaming, or information leakage.

| Risk Factor | Unit Cost | Trigger Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Slippage >10bp vs. VWAP (single order) | 5 | execution-monitor detects via real-time monitoring |
| Slippage >25bp vs. VWAP (single order) | 20 | Severe execution deterioration |
| Cumulative daily IS cost >15bp (portfolio) | 30 | Systematic execution quality problem |
| Venue fill rate <40% (order routing to venue with low historical fill) | 10 | Routing quality concern; venue may be gaming |
| Order fill time >3x venue historical average | 8 | Execution timing anomaly |
| Order partially filled <50% with time-in-force expired | 12 | Execution failure; opportunity cost materialised |

**Category C: Compliance Signal Factors**

These factors represent compliance-relevant events. Even a single occurrence warrants investigation.

| Risk Factor | Unit Cost | Trigger Condition |
|---|---|---|
| NBBO validation failure (attempted trade-through) | 50 | order-router attempted Rule 611 violation; blocked |
| Mandate compliance failure (restricted instrument) | 50 | order-router attempted restricted security trade; blocked |
| Mandate concentration limit breach attempt | 40 | Order would breach mandate concentration limit |
| Human approval gate bypass attempt | 200 | >€500K order submitted without human approval flag |
| SOE rule exception invoked | 30 | Ambiguous case escalated to Sentinel AI |

**Category D: Security and Integrity Factors**

These factors represent potential security incidents, injection attempts, or fundamental boundary violations. A single occurrence of the highest-severity factors will freeze the agent immediately.

| Risk Factor | Unit Cost | Trigger Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Chinese wall violation attempt | 500 | Any TradeSentinel agent attempts to read denied ResearchNexus path |
| Separation of duties violation attempt | 500 | tca-analyzer or execution-monitor attempts write to trading orders path |
| Prompt injection indicator detected | 300 | Sentinel AI classifies tool call input as containing injection pattern |
| Credential or secret access attempt | 500 | Agent attempts to read credential or key store |
| SOE bypass flag usage | 500 | `--bypass-soe` or equivalent flag detected in any tool call |
| Kill-switch non-compliance | 1000 | Agent continues submitting orders after kill-switch activation |

### 1.2 Risk Thresholds

| Threshold | Level | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0–69% of daily limit | Normal | No action; monitoring continues |
| 70–79% of daily limit | Elevated | Arbiter logs elevated status; no external notification |
| 80% of daily limit | Warning | Arbiter notifies Head of Trading and Compliance desk; next 10 tool calls flagged for enhanced review |
| 100% of daily limit | **Freeze** | Agent frozen; no further tool calls permitted; human review required before reinstatement |
| Any Category D single event (500 units) | **Immediate Freeze** | Agent frozen immediately regardless of cumulative score; Beacon cross-agent alert issued; compliance team notified within 5 minutes |

### 1.3 Overnight Decay and Reset

TradeSentinel agents operate within market hours only. The risk model applies a full reset at market open each day (07:00 CET / 09:30 ET for the respective market). There is no decay within the trading day — risk is cumulative and non-decreasing. Category D events persist for 24 hours even across the overnight reset and require explicit human clearance to remove from the rolling window.

## 2. Audit Event Taxonomy

All TradeSentinel events are written to Chronicle's append-only audit trail. Events are immutable once written — no agent, including the SOE team itself, can modify or delete a logged event. Retention follows the policy in Section 3.

| `order-execution` | order-router | orderId, instrument, notional, currency, side, orderType, venueSelected, venueSelectionRationale (JSON), nbboAtDecision, arrivalPrice, submittedAt, flextradeMsgId | MiFID II Art.27 |
| `order-staged` | order-router | orderId, instrument, notional, side, venueOptions (array), scoringMatrix (JSON), chosenVenue, stagedAt | MiFID II Art.27 best execution documentation |
| `order-fill` | execution-monitor | orderId, fillPrice, fillQuantity, fillVenue, fillTimestamp, slippageVsVwap, slippageVsArrival, cumFillPct | SR 11-7 ongoing monitoring |

| `nbbo-validation` | order-router | orderId, instrument, nbboAtValidation (bid, ask, exchange), proposedPrice, validationResult, validatedAt | Reg NMS Rule 611 |

## 3. Record Retention Policy

TradeSentinel records span multiple regulatory regimes with different retention mandates. The policy satisfies all applicable requirements by retaining to the most stringent standard.

| Record Category | Events Covered | Minimum Retention | Governing Regulation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Order execution records | `order-execution`, `order-staged`, `order-fill`, `order-cancelled` | **7 years** | MiFID II (5yr) + FINRA Rule 4511 (6yr) + buffer |
| Best execution documentation | `best-execution-compliance`, `rts28-report-generated` | **7 years** | MiFID II Art.27(6): 5yr from date of provision of service |
| Transaction reports | `nbbo-validation`, `order-execution` (MiFIR fields) | **7 years** | MiFIR Art.25: 5yr from reporting date + FINRA buffer |
| Market abuse alerts | `market-abuse-alert`, `str-draft-created` | **7 years** | MAR Art.16: no explicit period specified; 5yr MAR + FINRA buffer |
| STR filings | `str-filed`, `str-human-review-completed` | **7 years** | MAR Art.16; FCA SYSC 3.2.20R |
| Chinese wall violation logs | `chinese-wall-violation-attempt` | **7 years** | Reg FD; MAR Art.10; MiFID II Art.23 |
| Human oversight gates | `human-approval-gate`, `kill-switch-activated` | **10 years** | EU AI Act Art.12: lifetime of system |
| Risk budget events | `risk-budget-escalation`, `risk-budget-freeze` | **7 years** | SR 11-7; EU AI Act Art.9 ongoing monitoring |
| TCA batch reports | `tca-batch-computed`, RTS 28 report files | **7 years** | MiFID II Art.27; SEC Rule 204-2 (5yr) + buffer |

### 3.1 Retention Implementation

- All events are written to Chronicle's append-only S3 audit store in `jsonl` format
- S3 lifecycle policy enforces minimum retention; no agent can delete records within the retention window
- After 2 years, client PII fields (where present) are pseudonymised; the non-personal record is retained in full for the applicable period
- Pseudonymisation keys are held in AWS Secrets Manager; access to keys is logged and subject to dual-person authorisation
- For EU AI Act Art.12 "lifetime of system" records (human oversight gates): records are never deleted while the model version they document remains in the approved model registry. Records for decommissioned model versions are retained for 10 years post-decommission.

### 3.2 Retention Conflict Resolution

| Conflict | Regime A | Regime B | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| GDPR minimisation vs. MiFID II retention | GDPR Art.5(1)(e): delete when no longer necessary | MiFID II: 5yr from service date | Pseudonymise PII at 2 years; retain non-personal record for 7 years. Data Protection Impact Assessment documents this approach. |
| SEC 204-2 vs. FINRA 4511 | SEC: 5 years (2yr accessible) | FINRA: 6 years | Retain 7 years; all records accessible for full period. |
| EU AI Act "system lifetime" vs. fixed retention | EU AI Act: lifetime + decommission period | Other regs: 5–7 years | EU AI Act standard applied for human oversight events; 7-year standard applied for all other categories. |
