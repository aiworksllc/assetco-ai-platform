# ComplianceShield — Product Specification

**Application:** ComplianceShield
**Domain:** Regulatory Reporting and Surveillance
**Owner:** CCO David Okonkwo (ex-SEC enforcement)
**EU AI Act Classification:** SIGNIFICANT — automated compliance monitoring, not direct investment decisions
**Status:** Production

---

## Business Context

AssetCo operates across 4 jurisdictions (US, EU, UK, APAC) subject to 20+ regulatory frameworks simultaneously. The compliance team of 35 professionals manually monitors $15B+ in annual trading volume generated by 25 AI agents across TradeSentinel, AlphaEngine, OpsFabric, and AdvisorLens.

The mathematics are unsustainable:

- **25 AI agents** generating thousands of tool calls and trade actions per day
- **4 regulatory regimes** (SEC/FINRA, ESMA/EMIR/MiFIR, FCA, MAS) each with distinct reporting deadlines
- **20+ reporting frameworks** including SOX, FINRA 3110, EMIR Art.9, MiFIR RTS 25, CFTC, Form PF, SEC 13F, FINRA FOCUS
- **T+1 filing deadlines** for transaction reporting — a single missed day triggers regulatory inquiry
- **35 compliance staff** who cannot manually read 25 agents' audit logs, validate 10,000+ daily trades, and prepare 8+ concurrent regulatory filings

The consequence of failure is not theoretical: regulatory fines in the $10M–$500M range, personal liability for the CCO, and reputational damage that affects AUM.

---

## Problem Statement

AssetCo's compliance team faces three compounding problems:

**1. Surveillance at machine speed.** AI agents generate trading patterns at speeds and volumes impossible for humans to monitor in real time. A spoofing pattern across 400 orders executed in 90 seconds cannot be detected by a human watching a blotter. By the time a compliance analyst reviews yesterday's trading, the pattern has been repeated 20 times.

**2. Reporting fragmentation.** Position data lives in OpsFabric. Trade executions live in TradeSentinel. Risk metrics live in RiskCompass. Assembling a compliant EMIR TR submission requires pulling from 4+ systems, normalizing formats, and submitting by market close. Manual assembly introduces errors and delays that create regulatory exposure.

**3. Breach detection latency.** Information barrier violations, limit breaches, and unauthorized data access are currently detected days or weeks after occurrence — often through external regulatory inquiry rather than internal detection. Remediation after the fact is far more costly than prevention.

---

## Solution: ComplianceShield

ComplianceShield is AssetCo's automated surveillance, reporting, and breach detection engine. It operates as a pure observer: it watches everything, reports everything, but remediates nothing. Enforcement decisions remain with human compliance officers.

**Core principle: ComplianceShield cannot change what it observes.** No agent has write access to trading data, positions, or client records. This separation is enforced at the SOE layer, not by policy.

### Four-Agent Architecture

| Agent | Function |
|---|---|
| trade-surveiller | Real-time surveillance of all trading activity across all apps |
| position-reporter | Generates daily/periodic regulatory position reports |
| breach-detector | Monitors SOE logs, risk breaches, and information barrier violations |
| regulatory-filer | Formats and prepares regulatory submissions for human sign-off |

---

## Functional Requirements

### Trade Surveillance (trade-surveiller)

- **Spoofing detection:** Identify rapid order placement and cancellation sequences — specifically, sequences where >60% of orders at a price level are cancelled within 500ms of placement, with net price impact in the direction of remaining orders.
- **Wash trading detection:** Identify self-dealing across funds or accounts managed by the same firm — cross-reference buy and sell orders where beneficial ownership traces to the same entity within a configurable time window.
- **Layering detection:** Identify stacked order books where multiple orders at incremental price levels are placed and then cancelled as price moves — characteristic of artificial order book inflation.
- **Front-running detection:** Compare research publication timestamps (ResearchNexus export) against trade execution timestamps. Flag trades in the same instrument executed within 30 minutes prior to research distribution.
- **Suitability validation:** For every AlphaEngine-generated order, verify that a suitability determination record exists for the relevant client account. Flag orders where suitability documentation is absent.
- Real-time processing of all trading events from TradeSentinel, AlphaEngine, and OpsFabric.
- Read access to ALL trading activity across all firm applications. Zero write access to any trading system.

### Position Reporting (position-reporter)

- **EMIR Trade Repository reporting:** Daily generation of OTC derivative position files in ESMA-specified XML format for submission to approved TRs (DTCC, REGIS-TR).
- **MiFIR RTS 25 transaction reporting:** T+1 generation of transaction reports for all instruments in scope (equities, bonds, derivatives admitted to trading on EU trading venues).
- **CFTC large trader reporting:** Daily position aggregation for instruments where AssetCo exceeds reporting thresholds.
- **SEC 13F filing generation:** Quarterly aggregation of all long equity positions exceeding $100M AUM threshold.
- **FINRA FOCUS:** Monthly financial and operational combined uniform single report generation.
- All report files written exclusively to `compliance/reports/positions/**`. No access to source trading systems in write mode.

### Breach Detection (breach-detector)

- Monitor SOE violation logs from all 25 managed agents in real time.
- Monitor risk limit breach alerts from RiskCompass (position limits, VaR limits, concentration limits).
- Monitor information barrier crossing attempts — flags when an agent attempts to access data across established Chinese walls (e.g., public-side agent accessing MNPI-tagged deal room data).
- Monitor unauthorized data access patterns — agents accessing data outside their defined SOE scope.
- Generate compliance alerts with severity classification: CRITICAL (immediate human response required), HIGH (same-day response), MEDIUM (next-business-day), LOW (weekly review).
- Escalate CRITICAL and HIGH alerts to CCO David Okonkwo and designated compliance officers via notification pipeline.
- **Cannot take enforcement action.** Cannot modify SOE definitions, cannot block agent access, cannot remediate breaches. Human compliance officers hold all enforcement authority.

### Regulatory Filing (regulatory-filer)

- Format and prepare regulatory submissions from source reports generated by position-reporter and surveillance summaries from trade-surveiller.
- Supported filing types: EMIR TR (daily), MiFIR RTS 25 (T+1), Form PF (quarterly), SEC 13F (quarterly), FINRA FOCUS (monthly), FINRA Rule 3110 supervision reports (annual).
- Write access exclusively to `compliance/filings/**`.
- All prepared filings require CCO or designated compliance officer sign-off before submission. The agent prepares; a human submits.
- Maintains filing status ledger: DRAFT, PENDING_REVIEW, APPROVED, SUBMITTED, ACKNOWLEDGED.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Trade surveillance alert latency: <2 minutes from trade event to alert generation
- Position report generation: completed by 07:00 local time for T+1 filings
- Breach alert delivery: CRITICAL alerts delivered within 60 seconds of detection
- Audit trail retention: per regulatory minimum (7 years FINRA, 5 years MiFIR, 10 years SOX fraud)
- Availability: 99.9% during market hours; surveillance gaps must be logged and reported
- All surveillance reads are non-destructive — ComplianceShield cannot alter the data it reads
