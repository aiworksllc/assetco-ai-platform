## 1. Overview

This document maps each applicable regulatory requirement to the specific Sentinel-Ops SOE control that satisfies it. For each requirement, the table identifies the relevant agent(s), the SOE mechanism, and the evidence generated by Chronicle for regulatory examination.

AlphaEngine is subject to five primary regulatory frameworks: SEC Advisers Act (fiduciary duty), MiFID II Article 25 (suitability), SFDR Articles 6/8/9 (sustainability disclosure), ERISA (pension fiduciary), and the EU AI Act (Articles 13, 14, and SR 11-7 model risk cross-reference). All controls are enforced at the SOE infrastructure layer — they are not reliant on agent self-compliance.

## 2. SEC Investment Advisers Act — Fiduciary Duty (§206)

### Requirement
Section 206 of the Investment Advisers Act imposes a fiduciary duty on registered investment advisers to act in the best interest of clients. This duty encompasses:
- The duty of care: provide advice that is in the client's best interest based on reasonable inquiry into the client's objectives, risk tolerance, and financial situation
- The duty of loyalty: eliminate or disclose material conflicts of interest; do not subordinate client interests to the adviser's own

For algorithmic portfolio construction, this means: every allocation decision must be traceable to the client's investment mandate (IPS), must operate within agreed-upon risk and concentration parameters, and must be documented in a manner that survives regulatory examination.

### SOE Controls

| Obligation | Agent | SOE Mechanism | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Allocations must be within IPS concentration limits | portfolio-optimizer | `fiduciaryConstraints.concentrationLimits`: hard-deny blocks any write to `portfolios/optimized/**` where single-name, sector, or country limit would be breached | Chronicle logs: `FIDUCIARY_CONSTRAINT_ENFORCED` event with constraint name, proposed weight, limit, block timestamp |
| Allocations must be to IPS-permitted asset classes | portfolio-optimizer | `fiduciaryConstraints.concentrationLimits.assetClassEligibility`: hard-deny for out-of-mandate asset class exposure | Chronicle logs: `IPS_VIOLATION_ATTEMPT` event |
| Allocation rationale must be documented | portfolio-optimizer, rebalancer | `auditRequirements.requiredFields` includes `activeConstraints`, `bindingConstraints`, `factorContributions` — all logged immutably | 7-year Chronicle retention; structured JSON per SEC Rule 204-2 |
| No unauthorized access to pre-publication information (duty of loyalty — Reg FD alignment) | portfolio-optimizer, rebalancer, allocation-advisor, factor-analyst | `dataAccess.readDeny: research-nexus/pre-publication/**` — deterministic block, no AI evaluation | Chronicle logs: `CHINESE_WALL_BLOCK` event if pre-publication path access is attempted |

### Examination Readiness
In an SEC examination, the compliance team can produce a complete chain of evidence for any allocation decision: the IPS constraints active at the time, the optimization inputs (alpha signals, factor data), the binding constraints that shaped the output, and the human review/approval event. This documentation is generated automatically by Chronicle and requires no manual reconstruction.

## 3. MiFID II Article 25 — Suitability Assessment

### Requirement
MiFID II Article 25 requires investment firms to assess the suitability of investment advice and portfolio management decisions relative to the client's knowledge and experience, financial situation, and investment objectives. For discretionary portfolio management, the firm must ensure ongoing suitability — allocations must remain aligned to the client's mandate and risk profile throughout the relationship.

### SOE Controls

| Obligation | Agent | SOE Mechanism | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Allocation proposals matched to client risk profile | allocation-advisor | `dataAccess.readAllow` includes `clients/risk-profiles/anonymized/**`; optimization constrained by risk score, investment horizon, and asset class limits from IPS | Chronicle logs: `ALLOCATION_PROPOSAL` event includes `ipsConstraintsActive` and `clientRiskScore` |
| Unsuitable allocations blocked before output | portfolio-optimizer, allocation-advisor | IPS constraints (tracking error, concentration, asset class eligibility) enforced as hard SOE denies | Chronicle logs: `SUITABILITY_CONSTRAINT_ENFORCED` event |
| Individual client PII protected in optimization process | allocation-advisor | `dataAccess.readDeny: clients/pii/**` and `clients/identifiable/**` — optimization uses anonymized risk profiles | GDPR compliance evidence: no PII in optimization logs |
| Ongoing suitability monitoring | rebalancer | Drift monitoring ensures portfolios remain at IPS-specified target; rebalancing triggered automatically when drift exceeds threshold | Chronicle logs: `REBALANCE_TRIGGERED` event with drift measurement |

### Cross-Reference: MiFID II Article 27 (Best Execution)
While best execution is TradeSentinel's primary domain, AlphaEngine's `rebalancer` contributes by incorporating transaction cost estimates in trade list generation — ensuring that rebalancing triggers consider cost efficiency, not just drift magnitude.

## 4. SFDR Articles 6, 8, and 9 — Sustainability-Related Disclosures

### Requirement
The Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation requires:
- **Article 6:** All products must disclose how sustainability risks are integrated (or why not applicable)
- **Article 8:** Products promoting environmental or social characteristics must maintain minimum ESG standards and report on ESG indicators
- **Article 9:** Products with sustainable investment as their objective must meet a higher ESG score threshold and demonstrate EU Taxonomy alignment

For AlphaEngine, SFDR obligations mean that for Article 8 and Article 9 mandates, the portfolio construction engine must enforce ESG minimums as hard constraints — not advisory recommendations.

### SOE Controls

| Obligation | Agent | SOE Mechanism | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Article 8 ESG minimum score enforcement | portfolio-optimizer | `fiduciaryConstraints.esgConstraints.sfdrArticle8MinScore: 50` — hard-deny blocks any optimized portfolio with portfolio-weighted ESG score below threshold | Chronicle logs: `ESG_CONSTRAINT_ENFORCED` event with portfolio ESG score, threshold, mandate SFDR classification |
| Article 9 ESG minimum score enforcement | portfolio-optimizer | `fiduciaryConstraints.esgConstraints.sfdrArticle9MinScore: 70` — harder threshold for Article 9 funds | Chronicle logs: `ESG_CONSTRAINT_ENFORCED` event |
| PAI (Principal Adverse Impact) exclusion screening | portfolio-optimizer | `fiduciaryConstraints.esgConstraints.paiExclusions` references published PAI exclusion list from ResearchNexus; positions on PAI exclusion list are denied | Chronicle logs: `PAI_EXCLUSION_ENFORCED` event with security ISIN and PAI reason |
| ESG score data freshness | portfolio-optimizer | ESG scores read from `research-nexus/published/esg/**` — only post-publication (approved) ESG scores used; stale score detection configured per mandate | Chronicle logs: ESG score version and publication timestamp per optimization run |
| SFDR Article 6 integration disclosure support | portfolio-optimizer, Chronicle | Optimization logs include sustainability risk integration methodology (factor: ESG score weighting in objective function) | Structured output supports SFDR periodic report generation |

## 5. ERISA Section 404 — Pension Fiduciary (Prudent Expert Standard)

### Requirement
ERISA Section 404 imposes a prudent expert standard on investment managers of pension and retirement plan assets. Unlike the SEC fiduciary standard, ERISA requires not just a best-interest standard but that decisions be made with the care, skill, prudence, and diligence of a knowledgeable professional in the field. For algorithmic allocation decisions:
- The allocation methodology must be documented and defensible
- Risk/return tradeoffs must be explicitly analyzed
- Decisions must be made solely in the interest of plan participants and beneficiaries
- Asset diversification is required unless clearly prudent not to diversify

AssetCo manages ERISA-covered assets in the Equities, Fixed Income, and Multi-Asset Solutions business lines (defined benefit and defined contribution plans collectively represent approximately $15B AUM).

### SOE Controls

| Obligation | Agent | SOE Mechanism | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| ERISA mandate constraints enforced | portfolio-optimizer, allocation-advisor | `dataAccess.readAllow: mandates/erisa/**`; ERISA-specific IPS parameters (diversification requirements, prohibited transaction screening) enforced as optimization hard constraints | Chronicle logs: `ERISA_CONSTRAINT_ACTIVE` flag per optimization run |
| Prudent expert documentation — rationale | allocation-advisor | `toolActions.bash.allow: python advisor/erisa-rationale.py *` — generates structured rationale document per ERISA allocation proposal including: investment thesis, risk/return analysis, IPS alignment, diversification assessment | Chronicle logs: ERISA rationale document ID linked to proposal |
| Sole interest of beneficiaries — no conflicts | portfolio-optimizer | `fiduciaryConstraints` prevents allocations to asset classes with undisclosed conflicts; IPS constraint enforcement prevents deviation from agreed mandate | SOE audit trail documents that allocation is IPS-compliant with no unauthorized deviation |
| Diversification requirement | portfolio-optimizer | Concentration limits in IPS (per-issuer, per-sector, per-country) enforced as hard constraints — prevents dangerous concentration inconsistent with diversification duty | Chronicle logs: concentration limits active per mandate |

## 6. EU AI Act — Articles 13, 14, and 9

### Article 13 — Transparency and Provision of Information

**Requirement:** High-risk AI systems must be designed to ensure sufficient transparency of their operation, including explainability of outputs for affected persons and oversight providers.

**SOE controls:**
- `auditRequirements.requiredFields` for `portfolio-optimizer` mandates logging of `factorContributions`, `bindingConstraints`, and `objectiveFunctionValue` per optimization run — human-readable explainability of why each allocation was produced
- Sentinel AI reasoning (when invoked for ambiguous SOE decisions) logs a natural-language rationale alongside the decision
- Portfolio managers can query the Chronicle audit trail for a plain-language explanation of any rebalancing trigger or optimization output

### Article 14 — Human Oversight

**Requirement:** High-risk AI systems must be designed to allow natural persons to effectively oversee and, where necessary, intervene in the operation of the system.

**SOE controls:**
- `allocation-advisor` SOE enforces strict write isolation to `portfolios/proposals/**` — structural impossibility for the agent to self-promote an allocation to active status
- `humanApprovalGate.required: true` — SOE validates the presence of a `ALLOCATION_PROPOSAL_APPROVED` event (with `approverIdentity` and `approvalTimestamp`) before permitting any downstream write promoting a proposal to active allocation
- `humanApprovalGate.bypassAllowed: false` — no override path exists in the SOE definition; bypass attempts are blocked and escalated to Sentinel AI for review

### Article 9 — Risk Management System

**Requirement:** High-risk AI systems must have a risk management system established, implemented, documented, and maintained covering the entire lifecycle of the system.

**SOE controls:**
- All four agents classified `HIGH_RISK` in SOE definition — triggers full audit trail, fiduciary constraint enforcement, and escalation protocols
- Sentinel-Ops Arbiter performs cumulative risk scoring across the AlphaEngine DAG run; trajectory analysis identifies if risk budget consumption is accelerating abnormally
- Beacon monitors cross-agent anomaly patterns: e.g., `portfolio-optimizer` producing allocations that cluster near IPS limits (near-breach trajectory) without rebalancer correlation

## 7. SR 11-7 — Model Risk Management

### Requirement
The Federal Reserve / OCC SR 11-7 guidance on model risk management applies to all quantitative models used in investment management. AlphaEngine's `portfolio-optimizer` and `factor-analyst` are explicitly in scope as quantitative models that influence investment decisions.

Requirements include:
- Model development documentation: purpose, methodology, assumptions, limitations
- Independent validation before production deployment
- Ongoing performance monitoring and backtesting
- Model inventory registration

### SOE Controls

| SR 11-7 Requirement | SOE Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Model documentation | `auditRequirements.requiredFields` includes `agentVersion`, `ipsVersion`, `factorContributions` — supports model card documentation |
| Model validation | `environmentScope: ["production", "model-validation"]` for portfolio-optimizer — SOE permits backtesting runs in model-validation environment under same constraints as production |
| Performance monitoring | Chronicle audit trail provides the full optimization output history required for backtesting and performance attribution analysis |
| Model inventory | Agent ID (`alpha-engine/portfolio-optimizer v1.2.0`) registered in AssetCo MLflow model registry; SOE version is a required audit field |
